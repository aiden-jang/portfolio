# First-visit walkthrough

This walkthrough is prepared; it has not yet been conducted with fresh visitors
or physical phones. Automated browser checks do not establish whether a person
understands the portfolio.

Use someone who has not seen the site, starting with an actual phone and a fresh
browser visit. Let them choose their own route. Keep the task prompts below off
their screen, and avoid explaining the navigation or projects beforehand.

## Visitor tasks

1. Look around briefly, then describe what Aiden does in your own words.
2. Find a project that interests you. Explain what it does and what Aiden built.
3. Open its engineering story and describe one decision you understood.
4. Find the résumé, open it and return to the portfolio.
5. Find how you would email Aiden.

Record where they hesitate, what they miss and which words they use. Note whether
the garage helps them remember the site or keeps them from discovering the work.
Repeat with someone who reads engineering portfolios and someone who does not.

| Task                               | Observed route or hesitation | Change supported by the observation |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Explain the introduction           | Not tested                   | —                                   |
| Find and understand a project      | Not tested                   | —                                   |
| Understand an engineering decision | Not tested                   | —                                   |
| Open and return from the résumé    | Not tested                   | —                                   |
| Find email                         | Not tested                   | —                                   |

If visitors repeatedly miss the work, test one quiet “View selected work” link
beneath the introduction. A new button is a response to that observation, rather
than a change already justified by this walkthrough.

## Owner checks on real devices

Use Safari on an iPhone and Chrome on Android. Record the device, browser, network
and whether the visit was fresh or cached.

- Tap Menu while the first car loads; check that the tap gets a prompt response.
- Reach Projects and Contact while loading continues.
- Switch cars repeatedly, then background the browser and return.
- Open a case study and screenshot, then use Back to return.
- Check résumé opening, email and the native sharing sheet.
- Repeat the journey with reduced motion and the device’s screen reader.

Use the existing Speed Insights measurements alongside these observations.
Local CPU throttling, desktop WebKit and screenshots do not replace these checks.
