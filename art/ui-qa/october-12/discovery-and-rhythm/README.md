# Car discovery and reading continuity

The car name is a native model selector on desktop and phone. Every model is available
by name and chassis code, while phone arrows retain quick cycling. Keyboard focus is
visible on the selector wrapper. Loading temporarily disables selection.

Case studies remember reading position and expanded details while the page remains open.
Closing and reopening a story restores both; changing stories stores each independently.
Initial shared links still open at the beginning. Memory is local to the current page,
not persistent across reloads.

Experience and About use their content height rather than an unnecessary full-screen
minimum. Inspected their boundaries at 1440 and 390 widths. Contact retains its centered
arrival space and front-to-raised camera composition requested by the user.

Checked model selection at desktop 1440 and phones 390 and 320, reading-position and
expanded-detail restoration, and a bloomnote shared link requesting its composer view.
Reviewed screenshot presentation through the shared-link and resumed-story captures;
existing product views remain available without further crop changes.
