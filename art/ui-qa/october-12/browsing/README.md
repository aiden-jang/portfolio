# Browsing and contact pass

The featured projects are everythingisfine, bloomnote, mrrp, worth and iodine.
Owewell appears in the smaller gallery. Iodine is labeled as an open-source
contribution and links to authored commits; its native contribution panel deliberately
uses verified work in place of an unrelated IDE screenshot.

Only Intro and Contact accept car gestures. Reading backgrounds remain opaque during
car dragging, with a synchronous visibility guard during scroll and paused rendering
while reading. Contact returns the car with a lower camera composition.

Outcomes lead with four results, additional employer work is expandable, and open source
has a separate row. Filters expose counts and selected states. Screenshots have consistent
framing, useful captions and a full-image viewer; bloomnote uses a top crop in its preview.
About is left-aligned on phones and the gap into Contact is shorter. Paint and lighting
controls expose their current states, with visible paint labels on keyboard focus.

Browser checks cover desktop, tablet and narrow phones: fast scrolling, rejected reading
gestures, opaque backgrounds, the project lineup, expanded outcomes, filter retention,
case-study and image dismissal, and the returning Contact car. Captures were inspected.
