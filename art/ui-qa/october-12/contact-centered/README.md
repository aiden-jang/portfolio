# Centered Contact

Contact returns to centered copy in the middle of the viewport. The camera arrives in front of the car and rises into an elevated front view over a short arrival, replayed when returning to Contact. Reduced motion shows the settled view immediately. Intro keeps its existing idle spin and car interaction remains available in Contact.

A radial shade keeps the centered copy readable without introducing a card. Desktop and phone captures show arrival and settled framing; the phone capture uses reduced motion. Browser checks cover horizontal centering, the email link and overflow. The repository verification gate checks source changes.
