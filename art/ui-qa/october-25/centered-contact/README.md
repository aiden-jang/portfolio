# Centered Contact review

Contact centers the heading, supporting copy and email actions on desktop and
phones. A soft radial scrim keeps text readable over the car without changing its
materials or detail. Credits remain at the bottom. The camera keeps its arrival,
reversal and document-relative projection through the About transition. Short
phones frame the car closer to keep its silhouette visible around the copy.

The composition checks first rejected the previous left/top placement at each
reviewed size. They check horizontal and vertical centering, header/footer
clearance, reachable email actions and visible car pixels outside the copy.
