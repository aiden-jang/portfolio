# R8 replacement review

The Audi R8 widebody by wallon replaces the Countach in its existing garage slot.
The authored amber-gold paint, clearcoat, glass, lights and tires are retained.
Contact credits and picker attribution link to the creator and CC BY 4.0 license.

The official glTF source and compressed export were compared in the portfolio renderer.
Decoded vertex attributes, triangle topology, texture dimensions and RGBA pixels
match the source. No mesh simplification, texture resizing or material conversion
is applied. The license evidence record contains archive and asset hashes.

Desktop, tablet and narrow-phone viewport captures show the model and controls.
The night capture checks alternate paint without tinting the glass, tires or trim.
These captures show local browser rendering, not physical-device performance.
