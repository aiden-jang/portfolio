# Ferrari F1-75 garage review

The F1-75 replaces the 296 GT3 in the curated picker. Its permanent registry entry
is appended, preserving the retired 296's existing shared URLs. The F40 and RB16B
remain in the lineup. The F1-75 keeps its red livery, floor and wings, hides paint
controls, and has no synthetic road lights or invented animation.

[Clean dusk capture](dusk.jpg) supplies the picker preview. The normal
[desktop night view](desktop-night.jpg) and [phone dusk view](phone-dusk.jpg) show
the finished model in the portfolio. A linked blue paint request resolves back to
original racing colors. The picker selects the F1-75 and excludes the 296 GT3;
phone drag-to-orbit remains usable. Captures follow local-time lighting and are
not a controlled comparison of different material settings.

The [source-material diagnostic](source-materials.jpg) showed highly metallic red
paint and tires. Runtime corrections make livery, decals, carbon and rubber
dielectric, retain a restrained clearcoat on the body, and use rough tires. Texture
maps and colors, wheel hardware and geometry remain intact. Unit regressions guard
livery preservation, retired URL identities and material isolation. Temporary
repaint-enabled and metallic-paint mutations made those regressions fail before
the restored implementation passed.

The official full-resolution web export is losslessly compressed. Exact comparisons
preserve every selected-export triangle, decoded vertex attribute, texture dimension
and RGBA pixel, material definition and node. Decoded glTF validation reports no
errors or warnings. Original-upload and web-export triangle counts are distinguished
in the license review. The downloaded CC BY-NC-SA notice, attribution, adaptation
terms and publisher's provenance uncertainty accompany the asset and site credits.

Repository verification and focused Chromium/WebKit checks cover the replacement,
fixed livery, shared links, keyboard picker behavior and credits. These captures
use desktop browser viewport emulation; physical-phone GPU behavior remains untested.
