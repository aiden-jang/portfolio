# Surface refinements

The floor glow is smaller and dimmer, leaving the reflection visible without the broad
colored wash. Ferrari and Porsche mesh bounds were measured at floor level; their
positions were preserved. Shorter desktop intros use a smaller name and narrower copy
to reduce overlap with the car.

The car selector retains chassis codes and explicitly says Change car on both layouts.
Its desktop label and secondary text are larger and more legible. Contact keeps the
centered text and front-to-elevated camera movement, with stronger soft shading behind
copy and dark backing for social links, credits and keyboard hints.

The existing sticky project toolbar remains. Its offsets and project anchor clearance
were refined at desktop and phone widths. A project choice or filter now cancels an
in-progress section scroll so the earlier animation cannot override the new target.
The fast section-to-project sequence was reproduced before the fix and landed at the
correct offset afterward.

Garden and room previews use closer crops to make flowers and the cat visible. Garden
framing differs on phones to exclude the cut-off note card and retain the water control.
The image viewer still shows the complete original. Other product preview assets and
their full-image access are unchanged.

Native browser review covered desktop, shorter desktop and narrow layouts, sticky
toolbar offsets, fast project selection, and Contact with every car in both lighting
modes. The smallest layout had no horizontal document overflow. The repository
verification suite passed. Physical phone and Safari testing remain unavailable.
The résumé was not edited.
